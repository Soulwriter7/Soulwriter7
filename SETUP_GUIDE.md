### Step 2: Enable Contribution Snake GitHub Action
1. Go to: **[https://github.com/Soulwriter7/Soulwriter7/settings/actions](https://github.com/Soulwriter7/Soulwriter7/settings/actions)**
2. Scroll to **Workflow permissions**, select **Read and write permissions**, and click **Save**.
3. Go to the **Actions** tab: **[https://github.com/Soulwriter7/Soulwriter7/actions](https://github.com/Soulwriter7/Soulwriter7/actions)**
4. Click **"Generate Contribution Snake"** on the left menu, then click **"Run workflow"**.
5. Your custom purple snake will start crawling through your contributions on your live profile!
